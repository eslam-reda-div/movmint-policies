# Account Deletion Policy – Movmint

At **Movmint**, we respect your privacy and provide a straightforward way to permanently remove your account and its associated personal information.

## Steps to Delete Your Account

You can submit an account deletion request directly through the Movmint application by following these steps:

1. Launch the **Movmint** app on your device.
2. Navigate to the **Profile** section.
3. Find and select the **Delete Account** option.
4. Review the confirmation message and confirm your decision to proceed with permanent account deletion.

## Information Removed Upon Deletion

When your account is deleted, the following information will be removed from our systems:

- **Personal Details:** Your name, registered email address, and phone number.
- **Login Information:** Account credentials and authentication details.
- **Activity Records:** Previous trips, booking information, and reservation history.

## Important Information

Deleting your account is a permanent action that cannot be reversed. Once the deletion process is completed, you will lose access to your Movmint account, and the personal data associated with it will be permanently erased.
